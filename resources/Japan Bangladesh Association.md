# Japan-Bangladesh Association
**Friendship • Cooperation • Co-prosperity**
Building a stronger, richer, and more sustainable partnership between Japan and Bangladesh.

---

### **About Us**
**A bridge connecting two countries**
The **Japan-Bangladesh Association (JBA)** is a non-profit human exchange organization dedicated to strengthening friendship and cooperation between Japan and Bangladesh. It connects governments, businesses, universities, research institutions, civil society, and younger generations to create opportunities for cooperation that foster mutual understanding and long-term development.

---

### **Mission and Vision**
**Turning friendship into concrete action**
* **Mission**: To promote enduring friendship and cooperation between both nations through trade, education, culture, innovation, and people-to-people exchanges.
* **Vision**: To aim for a future where Japan and Bangladesh grow together as trusted partners, achieving inclusive and sustainable co-prosperity for future generations.

---

### **Key Focus Areas**
**Six pillars for a stronger partnership**:
1. **Economic Cooperation** — Trade, investment, and business collaboration
2. **Education & Human Resource Development** — Scholarships, skill development, and academic exchanges
3. **Cultural & People-to-People Exchange** — Exchanges respecting culture and diversity
4. **Technology & Innovation** — Digitalization, green technology, and startups
5. **Youth Leadership** — Fostering next-generation leaders
6. **Sustainable Development** — Creating an environmentally conscious, healthy, and resilient society

---

### **Japan-Bangladesh: A Growing Partnership**
Japan and Bangladesh have established a long-standing friendship built on mutual respect, trust, peace, development, and a shared dedication to human dignity. Together, both nations can build a stronger economy, deeper cultural connections, and a more inclusive, sustainable future.

**Connecting hearts and enriching lives**
